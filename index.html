Apprise AI Support

Need help with an appraisal, your saved collection, or an Apprise Premium subscription? Contact Aidan Roberts, the developer of Apprise AI.

Contact support

Email aidantroberts@gmail.com with your question or a description of the problem.

For technical issues, include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, app version, and the steps that led to the issue. A screenshot can help. Please do not send passwords or payment card details.

Subscriptions and purchases

Restore Premium: Open Apprise AI, tap the Settings gear on Home, then tap Restore purchases. Use the Apple Account that purchased the subscription.

Manage or cancel: On your iPhone or iPad, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and select Apprise AI. You can also use Manage plan in the app's Settings.

Request a refund: Visit Apple's Report a Problem website and sign in with the Apple Account used for the purchase.

Appraisals and saved items

For help with an identification, estimated value, or saved item, email the support address above. Appraisals are estimates and may differ from an item's actual sale price.
